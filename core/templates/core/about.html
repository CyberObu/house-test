About NyumbaHub

Our Story

Building Tanzania's Real Estate Future

NyumbaHub was founded to solve the challenges of Tanzania's real estate market — fragmented listings, unreliable information, and a lack of transparency.

We built a platform where buyers can search thousands of verified properties, agents can manage their business, and owners can list with confidence. Our goal is to make every property transaction in Tanzania smooth, transparent, and trustworthy.
