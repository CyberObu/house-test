Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how NyumbaHub collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform.

Contents

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use It 3. Sharing Data 4. Cookies 5. Security 6. Your Rights 7. Data Retention 8. Children 9. Policy Changes 10. Contact Us

NyumbaHub ("we", "our", or "us") is a real estate platform operating in Tanzania. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions, contact us at privacy@nyumbahub.co.tz.

1

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information automatically collected when you use our services.

Information You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, username, password, phone number, and profile photo when you register.
  • Property listings: Property details, photos, floor plans, documents, and pricing information you submit.
  • Communications: Messages, inquiries, tour requests, and reviews you send through the platform.
  • Payment data: Mobile money numbers and transaction references for plan subscriptions (we do not store full financial account details).

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, search queries, properties viewed, time spent, and click patterns.
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Location data: Approximate location inferred from your IP address to show relevant properties.
2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Create and manage your account
Display and promote your property listings
Send you inquiry notifications and tour confirmations
Process subscription payments
Send saved-search email alerts (if enabled)
Improve platform features and user experience
Prevent fraud and ensure platform security
Respond to your support requests
Send platform updates and policy notices

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

3

Sharing Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

With other users: Your public profile, property listings, and agent contact details are visible to other platform users as part of normal platform operation.
Service providers: We may share data with trusted service providers (email delivery, hosting, analytics) who are contractually bound to protect your information.
Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by Tanzanian law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of NyumbaHub, our users, or the public.
Business transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
4

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on NyumbaHub.

Cookie TypePurpose
EssentialSession authentication, CSRF protection, compare-list state
FunctionalSaved search preferences, language settings
AnalyticsUnderstanding how users interact with the platform (aggregated)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality.

5

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information including:

Password Hashing
CSRF Protection
Secure Servers
Access Controls

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information via your dashboard settings.
Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing privacy@nyumbahub.co.tz.
Opt-out: Unsubscribe from email alerts at any time via your Saved Searches dashboard or email footer links.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@nyumbahub.co.tz. We will respond within 30 days.

7

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account.
  • Property listings remain visible until unpublished or deleted by you.
  • Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by Tanzanian tax law.
  • Communications and inquiries are retained for 2 years.
8

Children's Privacy

NyumbaHub is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our platform or sending an email to your registered address. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of NyumbaHub after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
